Overall Goal

Create a website that feels significantly more premium than LP Advisory and Lux Buyers Agents, positioning Anchorfield as Australia's next-generation buyer's advocacy brand. The experience should inspire immediate trust, communicate financial expertise, and convert visitors into qualified consultation bookings while remaining scalable for future growth, suburb landing pages, blogs, calculators, testimonials, and case studies. Base the design direction on the business discovery questionnaire, including the founder's mortgage-broker background, nationwide focus, bold and modern brand personality, planned services, and emphasis on trust, expertise, and long-term SEO growth.
